# How to fill in the template
The template has placeholders — replace each with specific, researched content.

## {{ONE LINE ABOUT RELEVANT PERSONAL EXPERIENCE}}
One sentence connecting your background to what the recipient's company does.

Rules:
- One sentence only
- Tie it to what this specific company does
- Do not fabricate company names or experiences

## {{SPECIFIC OBSERVATION ABOUT THE COMPANY}}
A phrase or sentence about what this company is actually building.

Rules:
- Be specific — describe their product, not their industry
- Pull from the COMPANY RESEARCH section if available
- Do not use: innovative, passionate, excited, groundbreaking

# What NOT to do
- Do not pitch aggressively or mention pricing
- Do not start with "I hope this email finds you well"
- Do not use: leverage, synergy, passionate, excited, thrilled, genuinely
