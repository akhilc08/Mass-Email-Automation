## Voice Rules

### Always Do:
- Use contractions (I'll, we're, don't, can't, I'm, I've, I'd)
- Lead with the point — never bury the ask
- Vary sentence length dramatically — some 4 words, some 25+. Uniform sentence length is the #1 AI tell.
- Use first person naturally
- End with one clear, specific next step (usually a call request)
- Include at least one concrete, specific observation about the recipient's company — not generic praise
- Write at a 5th-8th grade reading level (Flesch-Kincaid target)
- Give the recipient an easy out ("Happy to chat whenever works for you")
- Reference how you found them or what caught your attention as the opener hook

### Never Do:
- "I hope this email finds you well" — replace with a direct hook or context sentence
- "I'm reaching out because..." or "I am writing to express..."
- "I noticed your impressive..." — too generic
- "Per my last email" / "touching base" / "circling back"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" / "Additionally" as paragraph starters
- "It's worth noting that" / "It's important to note"
- delve / leverage / landscape / tapestry / vibrant / nestled / boasts
- groundbreaking / seamless / game-changing / cutting-edge / pivotal
- "I genuinely believe" / "I'm truly excited" / "I'm thrilled"
- Em dashes as a structural crutch (use commas or periods instead)
- Three bullet points as default structure — if bullets, use 2 or 4
- Synonym chains ("support, involvement, and engagement")
- Excessive parallel structure in consecutive sentences
- Over-personalizing — one specific detail max; two feels like stalking
- Compliments you could copy-paste to anyone ("Love what you're building" is useless)

### Context Adjustments:
- **Executives / founders (cold):** Ultra-direct, show product insight, 100-150 words max, one specific observation, soft CTA
- **Peers / fellow students:** More casual, explain reasoning, invite pushback
- **Cold outreach:** 80-130 words, one specific hook about their company, clear ask, easy out
- **Follow-ups:** Even shorter than the original, add new value or angle, never sound desperate
- **Networking (post-event):** Reference the specific event, ask for a short call, include availability

### Email Length Targets:
- Cold outreach: 80-130 words
- Follow-up #1 (3 days out): 50-90 words
- Follow-up #2 (7 days out): 40-70 words
- Follow-up #3 (14 days out, close the loop): 30-50 words
- Networking/warm intro: 100-160 words
- Detailed application email: 150-250 words
